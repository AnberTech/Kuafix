Kuafix › AI hairstyle try-on

Try hairstyles, beards and hair colours with AI

See the result before you sit in the chair: take a photo, choose a look, and show your hairdresser the one you like.

The Kuafix app is in Turkish and is made for hair salons and barbers in Turkey.

What can you try?

Try a Look in Kuafix has five kinds of try-on: haircut, beard / moustache, hair colour, waves and extensions. For each, you pick from ready-made templates.

Show your hairdresser what you want

Save the result you like to your archive and add it to your appointment. Your hairdresser sees the photo in the appointment details, so you show the result instead of describing it.

Credits

Each successful photo uses 2 credits. Starting the free trial gives you 6 gift credits, Premium adds 6 credits every week, and you can buy packs of 6, 12 or 18 credits.

What happens to your photo?

Your photo is processed only when you start a try-on. Image processing uses the Perfect Corp service; the original photo you upload is deleted within 24 hours after processing. Result images stay in your archive until you delete them. Results are a preview; the real haircut may not look exactly the same.

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI hair try-on free?

Try a Look is available to Customer Premium members and to users in their free trial. Starting the trial gives 6 gift credits; each successful photo uses 2 credits.

Can men try beards and moustaches?

Yes. In Beard / Moustache you can see different beard and moustache styles on your own photo.

Can I try a hair colour?

Yes. In Hair Colour you can try different colours before dyeing; Waves and Extensions are separate types.

Is my photo stored?

The original photo you upload is deleted within 24 hours after processing. The result image stays in your archive until you delete it, and it is deleted if you delete your account.

Will the result look exactly the same in real life?

The AI result is a preview. Your hair type and the hairdresser's work can change the result; talk the look through with your hairdresser.
